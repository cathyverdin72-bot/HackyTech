# HackyTech

> You Think it. I Build it. We Make it digital.

HackyTech is a problem-first technology platform focused on turning real-world problems, ideas and business processes into practical digital solutions.

## What HackyTech Builds

HackyTech works across:

- Software
- Web Platforms
- Business Systems
- Data & Analytics
- Dashboards
- Automation
- APIs
- Intelligent Technology

The technology is chosen around the problem, the people using it and the outcome that needs to be achieved.

---

## Current Systems

### NouchiLink

A social connection platform designed around how people connect in Côte d'Ivoire.

### Feast & Fête

A digital food ordering experience supporting pickup and delivery.

### Lavage International

A business operations and vehicle tracking system for a car wash environment.

### SkillSwap

A platform concept focused on practical skill exchange and learning.
